## 1. The provenance defect (audit P2-D)

After the REWORK3 QA-FIX rebuild, `reports/rework3/r3_qa.md`
recorded the INTERMEDIATE zip (SHA `17781712…`, 285 entries,
MANIFEST 242) while the Drive file was the rebuilt zip (SHA
`1b0e4e8f…`, 286 entries, MANIFEST 243). The QA report was never
re-run against the final bytes. The R3 final report also
contradicted itself: its header said "independent QA verdict is
pending" while §13 said it had passed.

## 2. The fix

- `RELEASE_SHA256.txt` is written OUTSIDE the zip (next to it in
  `~/projects/` and uploaded to Drive alongside it) with the final
  SHA-256, the byte size, and the generation time. The zip never
  contains its own hash — no self-reference.
- The REWORK4 final report's header now states the QA status for
  THIS zip only: "the independent QA verdict for REWORK4 is
  pending (see §13) — the REWORK3 verdict
  (ACCEPTED_RESEARCH_PROTOTYPE_ONLY) applied to the REWORK3 zip
  only, not to this one." §13 is filled by the independent QA
  agent AFTER it verifies the final zip bytes (see `r4_05.md`).
- The independent QA for REWORK4 runs against the FINAL zip bytes
  (see `r4_05.md` §3) — not against an intermediate build.

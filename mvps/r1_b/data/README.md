# R1-B data — provenance

## `mitdb_100_10s.dat` / `.hea` / `.atr` (real recording excerpt)

- **Source:** MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database, record 100, downloaded
  2026-11-06 from https://physionet.org/files/mitdb/1.0.0/100.dat,
  `100.hea`, `100.atr` (PhysioNet, Goldberger et al.; freely available
  for research use with citation).
- **Excerpt:** first 10 s (samples 0–3599) of channel MLII, sampling
  rate 360 Hz, written with `wfdb.wrsamp`; annotations are the 14
  reference beats in that window, written with `wfdb.wrann`.
- **Why it is here:** the only REAL recording in R1-B. `real_upstream.py`
  reads it with real `wfdb` and processes it with real `neurokit2`
  (`ecg_process`), comparing detected R-peaks against the real
  annotations. Measured 2026-11-06: 12 peaks detected, 11/14 annotated
  beats matched within 100 ms (2 missed at the record edge, samples 18
  and 77; 1 offset by 133 ms), heart rate 72 bpm.
- **Size:** 7.2 KB + 100 B + 30 B. The full record (1.95 MB `.dat`) was
  downloaded to /tmp for slicing and is not shipped.

## `cases.json` (synthetic fixtures)

Generated by `make_fixtures.py` from the parameters stated in the cited
NeuroKit issues (4 kHz EMG, 8 s RSP at 15 breaths/min, RR period series
at 4 Hz). Synthetic — NOT the original recordings. See the R1-B README.
